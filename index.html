Turkey Names

  • Hens: Female turkeys; usually make softer chirping sounds instead of gobbling.
  • Toms: Male turkeys; known for gobbling.
  • Poults: A baby turkey is called a poult.
  • Flocks: A group of wild turkeys is called a flock.
